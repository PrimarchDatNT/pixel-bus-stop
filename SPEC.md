# Pixel Bus Stop — 4 mùa (style bible + asset contract)

A semi-static "lofi diorama" video: fixed far camera looking across a 2-lane Hanoi street at a
bus shelter whose roof is overgrown by a bougainvillea (hoa giấy). The base scene never moves;
seasons are told by swapping overlay layers, light, weather and the actors passing through.

Everything is **hand-made pixel art generated by Python scripts** (Pillow) — no AI images —
so every layer is separable, palette-locked and identical across seasons.

## 1. Canvas & geometry
* Logical canvas **480×270**, rendered at integer **4×** → 1920×1080. Never draw sub-pixels.
* All coordinates/baselines live in `tools/layout.py` — read it first, use its constants.
* Projection: flat side view with a slight top-down tilt (tops of sidewalks/road visible as
  bands). Buildings, shelter, vehicles, people = pure side view. No vanishing points.
* Look at `assets/out/_mock/_preview/scene@3x.png` for the blocking layout.

## 2. Palette & pixel rules (enforced by `tools/px.py: validate`)
* **Only** colors in `PALETTE` (Endesga 32 + 13 extras). Refer to them by name: `c("bloom")`.
* Alpha is 0 or 255. No anti-aliasing, no semi-transparent glows. Glows/fog/light cones are
  drawn as **dithered** patterns (`dither`, `vgradient`) on their own layer — the compositor
  may apply opacity at render time.
* Light comes from the **upper-left**. 3–4 tone ramps per material, hue-shifted (shadows cooler
  / more purple, highlights warmer). Avoid pillow shading and noise speckle.
* **Actors** (people, vehicles, animals) get a selective 1px dark outline (`ink`, or `brown_k` on
  warm objects) for readability. **Environment** (buildings, ground, sky) has no black outlines —
  edges use the darker shade of their own ramp.
* Background buildings are lower contrast and slightly cooler/desaturated (they're further away).
  Saturated reds/magentas are reserved for the hero (bougainvillea, Tết decor, signs, the bus).
* Do **not** bake ground shadows into sprites; the compositor adds soft shadow ellipses.
* **No baked glows** (rev 2): no dithered halos, light pools, light cones or fog dithers in any sprite —
  they read as checkerboard noise once composited. Emissive things export only their lit pixels
  (bulb, lantern body, lit window pane, moon disc); the compositor adds soft bloom/fog procedurally.
* Proportions: semi-realistic lofi, not chibi. Adult ≈ 28px tall, head 6–7px (≈4.3 heads).
  See scale guide in `layout.py` (bus 170×52, sedan 72×24, motorbike+rider 32×28 …).

## 3. Animation conventions
* Walk cycle 6 frames @ 8 fps with 1px head bob; run 6 frames @ 12 fps; vehicle wheels 2 frames
  @ 12 fps; idle loops 2–4 frames @ 3–4 fps; particles 4 frames.
* Canonical facing = **RIGHT** → name `*_R`. Provide `*_L` with `flip()` unless the sprite has
  text/asymmetry (then redraw). Far-lane traffic moves R→L (use `_L`), near-lane L→R (`_R`).
* Anchor (`save_asset(anchor=...)`): default bottom-centre = ground contact. Hanging things
  (lanterns, couplets, banners) → top-centre attach point. Say so in `notes`.
* All frames of one asset share one size. Multiple named states go in `states={...}` of a single
  strip (e.g. cat: walk / sit / lick / sleep).

## 4. Files & workflow (for every subagent)
* Scripts: `assets/src/<group>/*.py`; outputs only via `save_asset(group, name, ...)` →
  `assets/out/<group>/<name>.png|json` + `_preview/<name>@4x.png|.gif`.
* Run with the project venv: `.venv/bin/python assets/src/<group>/<file>.py` (cwd = project root).
  Script header to import the toolkit:
  ```python
  import sys, pathlib; sys.path.insert(0, str(pathlib.Path(__file__).resolve().parents[3]))
  from tools.px import *; from tools.layout import *; from tools.mockbg import mock_scene, place, save_mock
  ```
* Prefer ASCII `grid()` sprites for anything ≤ 48px (people, animals, small props) — it gives the
  most control. Use procedural `rect/line/poly/dither` + small grid details for big things.
* **Look at your output** (Read the `_preview/*@4x.png` / gifs) and critique it like a pixel
  artist: silhouette readable at 1×? clean lines (no jaggies/doubles)? coherent light? Iterate —
  at least 2 refinement passes on every hero/major asset.
* Do a placement check: `mock_scene()` + `place()` your main sprites at their layout positions,
  `save_mock(bg, group, "placement_check")`, view it, fix scale problems.
* Snake_case ASCII names. Season variants suffix `_xuan _ha _thu _dong`.
* Finish by writing `assets/out/<group>/MANIFEST.md`: one row per asset — name, frame size,
  frames/fps, anchor, layer (from `Z_ORDER`), suggested x/y, season(s), notes.
* Stay inside your own group folders. Don't edit `tools/`; report toolkit bugs in your final message.

## 6. Season beats (what the assets must support)
* **Xuân / Tết** — soft pastel morning, light drizzle (mưa bụi). Red lanterns under the shelter roof,
  a pair of câu đối (couplets) on the pillars, flags on balconies, "CHÚC MỪNG NĂM MỚI" banner, pots
  of quất & hoa đào, people in áo dài, motorbikes carrying peach branches / kumquat trees, swallows
  (chim én). Bougainvillea: green with first pink buds. Bàng: fresh light-green leaves.
* **Hạ** — bright noon + heat haze, bougainvillea in **full magenta bloom**, bàng lush green.
  A sudden downpour (mưa rào): sky darkens, heavy rain, splashes/puddles, riders in bat-wing rain
  ponchos (áo mưa cánh dơi), a pedestrian runs into the shelter holding a bag over the head; then the
  sun returns, wet glints, maybe a rainbow. Summer riders in áo chống nắng + khẩu trang.
* **Thu** — golden late-afternoon wind: gusts strip bougainvillea petals and yellow bàng leaves.
  Dusk → night: Trung Thu procession — kids with đèn ông sao (star lanterns) and carp lanterns,
  a múa lân (lion dance) troupe with drummer and Ông Địa, full moon rises, lantern string.
* **Đông (Hà Nội)** — grey-blue overcast, mưa phùn (fine drizzle) and fog bands, puffer coats &
  scarves, breath puffs, roasted corn/sweet-potato vendor with glowing charcoal + smoke, bicycle
  carrying white cúc họa mi daisies, bàng with sparse red leaves, bougainvillea sparse. Lamp turns on
  early. Ends with motorbikes carrying peach blossoms (Tết is coming) → seamless loop to spring.
* **Always**: buses stopping at the shelter, cars, motorbikes, pedestrians, birds crossing the sky
  / perching on wires, and the foreground cat (walks in, sits, licks paw, naps).
